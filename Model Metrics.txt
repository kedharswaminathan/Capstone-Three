CUSTOMER CHURN PREDICTION
CAPSTONE THREE - MODEL METRICS

Dataset:
Telco Customer Churn Dataset

Target:
Churn
0 = No Churn
1 = Churn

Train/Test Split:
80% Training
20% Testing

Models Evaluated:
1. Logistic Regression
2. Random Forest
3. Gradient Boosting

--------------------------------------------------
MODEL PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Logistic Regression
Accuracy:  80.38%
Precision: 64.94%
Recall:    56.95%
F1-Score:  60.68%
ROC-AUC:   83.61%

Random Forest
Accuracy:  78.32%
Precision: 61.31%
Recall:    50.00%
F1-Score:  55.08%
ROC-AUC:   81.98%

Gradient Boosting
Accuracy:  79.18%
Precision: 63.46%
Recall:    51.07%
F1-Score:  56.59%
ROC-AUC:   82.00%

--------------------------------------------------
SELECTED FINAL MODEL
--------------------------------------------------

Model:
Logistic Regression

Reason for Selection:
Logistic Regression produced the strongest overall
performance among the three models in the reported
test results. It achieved the highest accuracy,
precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC.
